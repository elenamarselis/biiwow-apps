What works offline

Works without internet

  • Opening the app, once it has been opened or installed online at least once.
  • Words and grammar of every level you have opened before: the app keeps a copy on the phone.
  • All games, flashcards, quizzes, grammar exercises and statistics.
  • Your progress: it is always saved on the device first, and sent to your account automatically when you are back online.
  • Pronunciation, as long as your phone has a voice for that language installed (most phones do).

Needs internet

  • The very first opening of the app, and opening a level for the first time.
  • Registering or logging in with your email.
  • Syncing progress between your phone and another device.
  • In Nine Scripts: the optional AI features (adding words from a photo, generating word lists).
  • Watching the YouTube lessons.

Tip: before a trip, open the app online once and tap through the levels you want to study. After that, everything you tapped works on the plane.

Save the apps on your phone or computer

Every app can be added to the home screen of an Android phone or iPhone, or installed on a computer, and then opens like a normal app with the biiwow icon. When you open an app for the first time it asks which device you use and shows the steps. You can see them here too.

How progress is saved

As a guest

No sign-up. Progress stays in this browser on this device only.

With an account

Register with an email and a password inside any Steps app or in Nine Scripts. Your progress then follows you to any phone or computer: log in with the same email and password. There is nothing to pay.
