# German Steps — content brief (biiwow app teaching German to Russian speakers)

Format: packs/VOCAB_SCHEMA.md and packs/GRAMMAR_SCHEMA.md in this folder. Validators: `node packs/vcheck.js <LEVEL>` and `node packs/gcheck.js <LEVEL>`.

## German-specific rules
Nouns WITH the definite article and capitalised: der/die/das ("der Tisch", "die Lampe", "das Buch"); give the plural in forms ("Pl. die Tische"; weak nouns also genitive: "des Kollegen, Pl. die Kollegen"). Verbs in the infinitive; give forms: 3rd person present, Präteritum, Perfekt with hat/ist ("fährt, fuhr, ist gefahren"); mark separable verbs with the separated present ("steht auf, stand auf, ist aufgestanden") and give the case for verbs with prepositions ("warten auf + Akk."). Adjectives: give comparative/superlative when irregular. IPA: standard German (Germany). note_ru: false friends with Russian, case government, Austrian/Swiss variants.

## Grammar sections per level (10 sections, in this order; ids kebab-case)

### A1
1. Pronunciation and spelling (ä ö ü ß, ei/ie, ch, sch, sp/st, final devoicing, word stress)
2. Nouns: gender der/die/das and plural forms; definite and indefinite articles
3. Personal pronouns; sein and haben
4. Present tense of regular verbs and stem-changing verbs (e→i/ie, a→ä)
5. Word order: verb in second position, inversion, W-questions and yes/no questions
6. Accusative case: articles, pronouns, prepositions für, ohne, durch, gegen, um
7. Negation: nicht vs kein
8. Possessive articles (mein, dein, sein, ihr, unser, euer, Ihr) in nominative and accusative
9. Modal verbs (können, müssen, wollen, dürfen, sollen) and möchte
10. Separable verbs and the imperative; numbers, time, dates

### A2
1. Perfekt with haben and sein; regular and irregular Partizip II
2. Präteritum of sein, haben and the modal verbs
3. Dative case: articles, pronouns, dative verbs (helfen, gefallen, gehören, danken)
4. Dative prepositions and two-way prepositions (Wechselpräpositionen): wo? vs wohin?
5. Reflexive verbs (sich freuen, sich waschen, sich die Hände waschen)
6. Adjective endings 1: after definite and indefinite articles in nominative and accusative
7. Comparative and superlative (größer als, so groß wie, am größten, gut – besser – am besten)
8. Subordinate clauses with weil, dass, wenn, ob: verb at the end
9. Coordinating conjunctions (und, aber, oder, denn, sondern) and adverbs deshalb, trotzdem, dann
10. Prepositions of time (am, im, um, vor, nach, seit, bis) and es gibt; werden as a full verb

### B1
1. Präteritum of regular and irregular verbs in narration
2. Plusquamperfekt; nachdem, bevor, als vs wenn
3. Konjunktiv II in the present: würde, wäre, hätte, könnte; polite requests, wishes, advice
4. Conditional sentences: real and unreal in the present (wenn … dann)
5. Relative clauses in nominative, accusative and dative; was and wo as relative words
6. Passive (Vorgangspassiv) in present and past; passive with modal verbs
7. Genitive case and genitive prepositions (wegen, trotz, während, statt)
8. Infinitive with zu; um … zu, ohne … zu, statt … zu
9. Adjective endings 2: all cases, adjectives without article, adjectives as nouns
10. Verbs with prepositions and pronominal adverbs (darauf, worauf, damit)

### B2
1. Konjunktiv II in the past (hätte gemacht, wäre gegangen); als ob, als wenn
2. Passive advanced: Zustandspassiv, passive in Perfekt, alternatives (man, sich lassen, -bar, sein + zu)
3. Konjunktiv I and indirect speech
4. Futur I and Futur II; expressing assumptions (wird wohl …, wird … haben)
5. Two-part conjunctions (sowohl … als auch, weder … noch, entweder … oder, nicht nur … sondern auch, je … desto)
6. Subordinate clauses advanced: obwohl, damit vs um … zu, indem, sodass, falls, sobald, solange
7. Participles as adjectives (der lachende Mann, das gekaufte Auto) and extended attributes
8. Modal verbs in subjective meaning (er soll reich sein, sie muss krank sein, er will es gesehen haben)
9. Nominalisation and the nominal style (die Durchführung, zur Verfügung stellen)
10. Word order in the middle field (TeKaMoLo, pronouns before nouns, nicht position), the Nachfeld and emphasis

### C1
1. Funktionsverbgefüge (in Frage kommen, zur Sprache bringen, Kritik üben)
2. Extended participial attributes and turning them into relative clauses
3. Modal particles (doch, ja, mal, eben, halt, schon, wohl, denn)
4. Formal genitive prepositions (angesichts, aufgrund, hinsichtlich, mittels, zwecks, ungeachtet)
5. Passive substitutes and impersonal constructions (es wird gesagt, lässt sich, das zu lösende Problem)
6. Verb prefixes: separable vs inseparable (übersetzen, umfahren, durchschauen) and meaning changes
7. Konjunktiv I and II in the news and academic writing
8. Word formation: compounds, linking -s-, suffixes (-ung, -heit, -keit, -schaft, -bar, -los)
9. Text cohesion: connectors (zumal, insofern, folglich, hingegen), reference and pronominal adverbs
10. Register: written vs spoken German, colloquial forms, Austrian and Swiss differences

### C2
1. Literary and archaic forms: Konjunktiv I in literature, genitive objects (sich einer Sache bedienen), dative -e
2. Complex sentence architecture: nested clauses and the verbal bracket in long sentences
3. Modality and evidentiality (dürfte, mag, soll as hearsay, angeblich, vermeintlich)
4. Nuances of the passive: von vs durch, bekommen-passive (Rezipientenpassiv)
5. Fine points of case: apposition, als/wie phrases, measurements, genitive vs von
6. Word formation advanced: be-/ver-/zer-/er-/ent- verbs, conversion, derivation
7. Orthography and punctuation: spelling reform, comma rules, capitalising nominalisations, ss vs ß
8. Prepositional regime (sich berufen auf, verzichten auf, sich entziehen + Dativ)
9. Discourse and pragmatics: hedging, politeness, irony, du vs Sie today
10. Variation and advanced errors: dialects, Austria and Switzerland, wegen + Dativ, weil + main-clause order, tun-periphrasis

Each grammar section: 6-10 rules with 2-4 examples each; 24-32 exercises mixing mc, fill, order, tf, match (about 35/30/15/10/10%), easy to hard; Russian explanations (always containing Russian words) that point out contrasts with Russian; vary the position of the correct mc option; options of an mc item must differ by more than capitalisation.

## How to work
Write in parts with the Write tool into a unique subfolder of your scratchpad (random suffix; the scratchpad is shared with other agents). After EACH part, concatenate what exists into the pack file with a small node script, so partial work is never lost. Run the validator until it prints OK (vocab) or no ERRORS (grammar), and re-run it right before you report, because other levels are written in parallel.
