# Italian Steps — content brief (biiwow app teaching Italian to Russian speakers)

Format: packs/VOCAB_SCHEMA.md and packs/GRAMMAR_SCHEMA.md in this folder. Validators: `node packs/vcheck.js <LEVEL>` and `node packs/gcheck.js <LEVEL>`.

## Italian-specific rules
Nouns WITH the article: il/lo/la/l'/i/gli/le ("il libro", "lo zaino", "l'amica"). Verbs in the infinitive; irregular verbs give key forms in "forms" (present io/tu/lui, participle, passato remoto from B2). Adjectives give feminine and plural. IPA: standard Italian, mark stress and gemination (ˈkaːza, ˈpalla). note_ru: false friends with Russian, essere/avere as auxiliary, prepositions, regional variants.

## Grammar sections per level (10 sections, in this order; ids kebab-case)

### A1
1. Pronunciation and spelling (c/g, gli, gn, sc, double consonants, stress)
2. Gender and number of nouns; articles il/lo/la/l'/i/gli/le, un/uno/una
3. Essere and avere; c'è / ci sono
4. Present tense of regular -are, -ere, -ire verbs (incl. -isc-)
5. Common irregular verbs in the present (andare, fare, venire, volere, potere, dovere, stare, dire, uscire)
6. Adjectives: agreement and position; possessives with articles
7. Questions and negation
8. Simple and articulated prepositions (di, a, da, in, su + article)
9. Piacere and indirect pronouns mi, ti, gli, le
10. Numbers, time, dates; reflexive verbs; stare per

### A2
1. Passato prossimo with avere and essere; participle agreement
2. Irregular past participles
3. Imperfetto and imperfetto vs passato prossimo
4. Futuro semplice; polite condizionale
5. Direct and indirect object pronouns, combined pronouns (glielo), position with infinitives
6. Ci and ne
7. Stare + gerundio, stare per, appena, già
8. Comparatives and superlatives (più/meno di/che, -issimo, migliore/peggiore)
9. Imperative tu/Lei/voi, affirmative and negative, with pronouns
10. Indefinites and quantifiers (qualche, alcuni, nessuno, niente, ogni, tutto); già/ancora/mai

### B1
1. Congiuntivo presente: forms
2. Congiuntivo uses 1: opinion, emotion, will, doubt vs indicative
3. Congiuntivo uses 2: conjunctions (perché, affinché, benché, prima che) and impersonal expressions
4. Trapassato prossimo and the past tenses in narration
5. Condizionale presente and passato (incl. future in the past)
6. Periodo ipotetico 1 and 2 with congiuntivo imperfetto
7. Discorso indiretto: statements, questions, commands
8. Relative pronouns: che, cui, il quale, chi, quello che
9. Passive and impersonal: essere/venire/andare + participle, si passivante, si impersonale
10. Gerundio, infinito and connectors

### B2
1. Congiuntivo passato and trapassato; concordanza dei tempi
2. Periodo ipotetico 3 and mixed
3. Concessive, conditional and manner clauses (sebbene, purché, a patto che, come se, senza che)
4. Congiuntivo in relative clauses and after superlatives
5. Opinion, doubt and probability: futuro anteriore of probability, dovere epistemic
6. Passato remoto: forms and use in narrative
7. Pronominal verbs and idioms with ci/ne (farcela, andarsene, metterci, volerci)
8. Participle and gerund clauses
9. Discorso indiretto advanced: backshift, time and place changes, reporting verbs
10. Text organisation: connectors, discourse markers, word order

### C1
1. Futuro anteriore and condizionale passato: anteriority, journalistic conditional
2. Congiuntivo in independent clauses (magari, che + congiuntivo)
3. Verbs that change meaning with indicative vs congiuntivo
4. Infinitive constructions: infinito sostantivato, fare/lasciare + infinitive
5. Verbal periphrases (andare + participio, venire + participio, stare a + infinitive, mettersi a, finire per)
6. Article nuances: possessives with family, geographic names, omission
7. Advanced prepositions: uses of da, a vs in with places, prepositional verbs
8. Emphasis: dislocations (Marco l'ho visto), frasi scisse (è lui che), proprio
9. Impersonality: si + uno, ci si, bisogna, occorre
10. Colloquial grammar and register: che polivalente, mica, dai, allora; diminutives and augmentatives

### C2
1. Literary tenses: passato remoto and trapassato remoto in literature
2. Congiuntivo in fixed formulas (sia come sia, che io sappia, costi quel che costi)
3. Modality and evidentiality (pare che, a quanto pare, sembrerebbe, dovrebbe essere)
4. Syntax of complex sentences: correlatives, consecutives, comparatives with clauses (più di quanto + congiuntivo)
5. Pronominal syntax edge cases: clitic order, prendersela, cavarsela, aspectual si (mangiarsi)
6. Word formation: prefixes s-/dis-/ri-/stra-, alterations, parasynthetic verbs
7. Orthography and punctuation: accents, po', qual è, d eufonica, apostrophes
8. Prepositional regime (badare a, dipendere da, consistere in, rinunciare a)
9. Discourse and pragmatics: mitigation, politeness, irony, insomma, cioè, comunque
10. Variation and advanced errors: regional Italian, passato remoto vs prossimo by region, a me mi, gli for le, se + condizionale

Each grammar section: 6-10 rules with 2-4 examples each; 24-32 exercises mixing mc, fill, order, tf, match (about 35/30/15/10/10%), easy to hard; Russian explanations (always containing Russian words) that point out contrasts with Russian; vary the position of the correct mc option; options of an mc item must differ by more than capitalisation.

## How to work
Write in parts with the Write tool into a unique subfolder of your scratchpad (random suffix; the scratchpad is shared with other agents). After EACH part, concatenate what exists into the pack file with a small node script, so partial work is never lost. Run the validator until it prints OK (vocab) or no ERRORS (grammar), and re-run it right before you report, because other levels are written in parallel.
