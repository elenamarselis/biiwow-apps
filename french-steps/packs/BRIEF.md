# French Steps — content brief (Biiwow app teaching French to Russian speakers)

Format: packs/VOCAB_SCHEMA.md and packs/GRAMMAR_SCHEMA.md in this folder. Validators: `node packs/vcheck.js <LEVEL>` and `node packs/gcheck.js <LEVEL>`.

## French-specific rules
Nouns WITH the article: le/la/l'/les ("le livre", "la maison", "l'école"; for l' nouns give the gender in forms: "f., pl. les écoles"). Verbs in the infinitive; irregular verbs give key forms in "forms" (je/tu/il present, participle, auxiliary être where used). Adjectives give feminine and plural. IPA: standard French (Paris). note_ru: false friends with Russian, être vs avoir as auxiliary, liaison, Belgian/Swiss/Québec variants.

## Grammar sections per level (10 sections, in this order; ids kebab-case)

### A1
1. Pronunciation and spelling (accents, silent letters, liaison, élision, nasal vowels)
2. Gender and number of nouns; articles le/la/l'/les, un/une/des, partitive du/de la
3. Être and avoir; il y a; c'est vs il est
4. Present tense of -er verbs
5. Irregular verbs in the present (aller, faire, venir, vouloir, pouvoir, devoir, prendre, dire, sortir) and -ir/-re verbs
6. Adjectives: agreement and position (beau/bel); possessives
7. Questions (est-ce que, inversion) and negation (ne…pas, jamais, plus, rien)
8. Prepositions of place, à/de + article (au, du), countries and cities
9. Aimer, adorer, préférer; numbers, time and dates
10. Futur proche (aller + infinitive) and pronominal verbs

### A2
1. Passé composé with avoir and être; agreement
2. Irregular past participles
3. Imparfait and imparfait vs passé composé
4. Futur simple; polite conditionnel
5. Object pronouns COD/COI, order, position with infinitives
6. The pronouns y and en
7. Être en train de, venir de, aller + infinitive
8. Comparatives and superlatives (meilleur, mieux)
9. Imperative affirmative and negative, with pronouns
10. Indefinites (quelque, aucun, personne, rien, chaque, tout); déjà, encore, toujours, ne…plus

### B1
1. Subjonctif présent: forms
2. Subjonctif uses 1: will, emotion, doubt; il faut que
3. Subjonctif uses 2: conjunctions (pour que, bien que, avant que, à condition que)
4. Plus-que-parfait and the past tenses in narration
5. Conditionnel présent and passé
6. Hypothesis: si + présent, si + imparfait
7. Discours indirect: statements, questions, commands
8. Relative pronouns: qui, que, où, dont, lequel; ce qui / ce que
9. Passive and alternatives: être + participle, se faire, on
10. Gérondif, participe présent and connectors

### B2
1. Subjonctif passé; sequence of tenses
2. Si + plus-que-parfait and mixed conditionals
3. Concession, condition and manner (bien que, quoique, pourvu que, comme si, sans que)
4. Subjonctif in relative clauses (le seul qui, je cherche quelqu'un qui)
5. Opinion, doubt and probability: je ne pense pas que + subjonctif, devoir epistemic, futur antérieur of probability
6. Passé simple: recognising and reading it
7. Advanced pronouns: en/y idioms (s'en aller, s'y prendre, en vouloir à), pronoms toniques
8. Agreement of the past participle: advanced cases
9. Discours indirect advanced: backshift, time and place changes, reporting verbs
10. Text organisation: connectors, discourse markers, word order

### C1
1. Futur antérieur and conditionnel passé (journalistic conditional)
2. Subjonctif nuances: que + subjonctif, quoi que / quoique, qui que
3. Verbs that change meaning with indicative vs subjonctif (dire que, comprendre que, admettre que)
4. Infinitive constructions: faire/laisser + infinitive, se faire + infinitive, infinitif passé
5. Verbal periphrases (être sur le point de, se mettre à, finir par, ne faire que, avoir beau)
6. Article nuances: omission, de after negation and before adjectives, partitive edge cases
7. Advanced prepositions: en vs dans, à vs de with verbs, prepositional verbs
8. Emphasis: mise en relief (c'est… qui/que, ce qui… c'est), dislocation
9. Impersonality: on, il s'agit de, il vaut mieux, il paraît que
10. Colloquial grammar and register: dropped ne, ça, quoi, genre, verlan basics, tu vs vous

### C2
1. Literary tenses: passé simple and passé antérieur; imparfait and plus-que-parfait du subjonctif
2. Subjonctif in fixed formulas (soit, vive, coûte que coûte, que je sache, advienne que pourra)
3. Modality and evidentiality (il semblerait que, journalistic conditional, sans doute vs sûrement)
4. Syntax of complex sentences: plus… plus, autant… autant, si bien que, de sorte que + indicative/subjonctif
5. Pronominal edge cases: pronoun order with the imperative, ne explétif, en/y in fixed expressions
6. Word formation: prefixes, suffixes, derivation, nominalisation
7. Orthography and punctuation: participle agreement edge cases, hyphens, capitals, 1990 spelling reform
8. Prepositional regime (pallier, se souvenir de, se rappeler, s'attendre à ce que)
9. Discourse and pragmatics: mitigation, politeness, irony, reformulation (autrement dit, bref)
10. Variation and advanced errors: France/Belgium/Switzerland/Québec; si j'aurais, après que + indicative, malgré que

Each grammar section: 6-10 rules with 2-4 examples each; 24-32 exercises mixing mc, fill, order, tf, match (about 35/30/15/10/10%), easy to hard; Russian explanations (always containing Russian words) that point out contrasts with Russian; vary the position of the correct mc option; options of an mc item must differ by more than capitalisation.

## How to work
Write in parts with the Write tool into a unique subfolder of your scratchpad (random suffix; the scratchpad is shared with other agents). After EACH part, concatenate what exists into the pack file with a small node script, so partial work is never lost. Run the validator until it prints OK (vocab) or no ERRORS (grammar), and re-run it right before you report, because other levels are written in parallel.
